About me

Good systems begin with better questions.

Curious about technology. Focused on the people who use it.

I'm an Information Systems student interested in the space between business needs and technology. I enjoy understanding workflows, organizing requirements, imagining practical web solutions, and checking whether features work as intended.

My academic explorations range from management systems and service platforms to mapping tools and analytics. I'm always working to turn concepts into thoughtful, usable experiences.
